# Code of conduct

beekeep follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and criticise ideas, never people. Harassment, insults and personal attacks have no place here, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented.

If something happens that you want to report, contact the maintainer, [@ASRIDK](https://github.com/ASRIDK), privately. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove comments or block people who break these rules.
